# Vamela Brand Guidelines & Marketing Ecosystem

## 1. Brand Strategy & Identity
**Company:** Vamela
**Industry:** Web Design & Digital Solutions
**Target Audience:** Small to medium-sized businesses, startups, and entrepreneurs seeking a professional, high-converting digital presence without the typical agency overhead.

### Narrative & Archetype
**Archetype:** The Creator / The Magician
Vamela transforms complex digital challenges into elegant, high-performing websites. We believe that a powerful online presence should be accessible, transparent, and built on a foundation of trust and expertise.

### Tone of Voice Matrix
*   **Professional, but not Corporate:** We speak with authority but remain approachable.
*   **Innovative, but not Jargon-heavy:** We use modern technology but explain it simply.
*   **Empowering, but not Overpromising:** We focus on real results and transparent processes.

## 2. Visual Identity & Color System
Our color system is built on a foundation of trust (Blues) and clarity (Neutrals), with strategic semantic colors for conversion.

*   **Primary (Trust & Action):** Blue 600 (`#2563eb`)

    *   *CMYK:* 85, 50, 0, 0
    *   *RGB:* 37, 99, 235
*   **Neutral (Clarity & Structure):** Slate 900 (`#0f172a`)

    *   *CMYK:* 65, 43, 26, 78

*   **Background (Space & Focus):** White (`#ffffff`) and Slate 50 (`#f8fafc`)

## 3. Typography Scale (9 Levels)
We use a 9-level typography scale to ensure perfect hierarchy, readability, and accessibility across all devices.

1.  **Level 9 (Display):** 4.5rem (72px) - Hero Headlines
2.  **Level 8 (H1):** 3.75rem (60px) - Page Titles
3.  **Level 7 (H2):** 3rem (48px) - Section Headers
4.  **Level 6 (H3):** 2.25rem (36px) - Sub-section Headers
5.  **Level 5 (H4):** 1.875rem (30px) - Card Titles
6.  **Level 4 (Body Large):** 1.25rem (20px) - Intro Paragraphs
7.  **Level 3 (Body Base):** 1rem (16px) - Standard Text
8.  **Level 2 (Body Small):** 0.875rem (14px) - Secondary Text
9.  **Level 1 (Caption):** 0.75rem (12px) - Legal, Metadata

## 5. UI/UX & Figma Auto-Layout Principles
*   **8px Grid System:** All padding, margins, and sizing must be multiples of 8 (e.g., 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64).
*   **Auto-Layout:** Every component must use Auto-Layout. No fixed heights or widths unless strictly necessary for icons or specific media.
*   **Accessibility:** All text must meet WCAG AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text). Focus states must be clearly visible (e.g., `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-primary focus-visible:ring-offset-2`).
*   **Micro-interactions:** Use subtle scaling (`scale-105`) and opacity changes on hover. Use spring animations for modals and popups.
